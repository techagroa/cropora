# CROPORA

**Agricultural Management and Support System for Farmers**

## Problem

Farmers often have difficulty finding the right information when they need it. They may struggle to get reliable weather updates, keep track of farm expenses and income, find buyers, or access financial and government support.

These problems can be harder in rural areas where internet access is limited. Farmers may also need to use different sources to find the information and services they need, which can be time-consuming and confusing.

## Solution

**CROPORA** is a simple and user-friendly mobile application that brings useful farming information and services together in one place.

Farmers can use CROPORA to:

* Manage their profile.
* Record income and expenses with the **Farm Ledger**.
* Check weather updates and farming advisories.
* Learn about farming through the **Agricultural Knowledge Hub**.
* Find government and financial support.
* Connect with potential buyers.
* Submit and track complaints or grievances.
* Share knowledge and experiences with other farmers.
* Use important features even with a poor internet connection.
* Use the app in local and regional languages.

CROPORA is designed to make everyday farm management easier, even for farmers who have limited access to the internet or digital services.

## Objectives

* Make farming information easy to find and understand.
* Help farmers manage their income and expenses.
* Provide useful weather updates and advisories.
* Make farming knowledge easily accessible.
* Help farmers find support and financial resources.
* Help farmers connect with buyers.
* Make submitting and tracking grievances simple.
* Provide a community for farmers to share knowledge.
* Support farmers with limited internet access.
* Make the app simple and accessible through local and regional languages.
